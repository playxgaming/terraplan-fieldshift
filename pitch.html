02 / 07 · The local question

What changes when winter water is limited?

Our editable Jobner case starts with bajra → wheat → Bare on 2 hectares. A farmer can examine the current plan, compare two alternatives and see the assumptions behind each result.

Location: 26.97° N, 75.38° E
Scenario: broad Loam class, well, 150 mm rabi irrigation
Not a surveyed farm or measured well capacity.

Local context: SKN Agriculture University thesis (2021); ICAR-CRIDA Jaipur contingency plan (2011). Dated references are linked in slide 7.

03 / 07 · How NASA data enter the decision

History → crop windows → explained comparisons.

NASA POWER

Daily rainfall and temperature; model soil wetness. Four crop years: June 2022–May 2026. Monthly history: 1991–2024.

Farm + crop inputs

Soil category, irrigation, equipment, current crops and six priority weights. Fifteen unverified crop profiles, including three source-researched additions.

Transparent rules

Calendar checks, climate fit, water gaps and crop-family rules. Every exclusion has a reason; all starter confidence is Low.

NASA POWER is a coarse area dataset, not a field sensor. Its soil wetness is model-derived. The animated 3D farm is an illustration.

05 / 07 · A farmer stays in control

Inspect. Adjust. Discuss locally.

  1. Run the Jaipur demo, then open a month in its crop timeline.
  2. Adjust water or soil-health priorities and inspect the new ranking.
  3. Test a drier or warmer historical scenario on the same three plans.
  4. Review four-year sensitivity; print or download a decision brief for a KVK discussion.

Mobile layout, crop photos, glossary, optional map and regional crop labels support exploration. The local bundled demo works with a local server without venue internet.

Experiments are sensitivity checks, not forecasts. Local storage keeps field, priorities and What-if settings separately.

06 / 07 · What is proven, and what is next

Working software. Validation remains.

Implemented

Seven screens, sourced NASA records, current/A/B comparison, explicit exclusions, priorities, timelines, sensitivity, local export and print.

Known gaps

Crop inputs are unverified. No field trial or farmer-outcome evidence. No measured soil test, local prices or satellite summaries. Mustard, barley and guar are included, with local validation pending.

Next validation

Have a local agronomist review crop parameters and varieties. Test actual irrigation capacity. Pilot with farmers and validate against field records before recommendations.

No guaranteed water saving, yield gain, profit increase, institutional partnership or endorsement is claimed.
